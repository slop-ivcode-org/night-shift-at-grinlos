How to Play

Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM in the security office. The animatronics roam at night and try to reach your two doors.

  • SPACE / S: raise or lower the camera monitor. Click the map to switch cameras, or use the arrow keys.
  • A / D: close or open the left and right doors.
  • Q / E: turn on the left or right hall light to see who is in the doorway.
  • Each thing you use drains power. If power runs out... good luck.
  • Patches comes down the west (left) hall. Clank comes down the east (right) hall.
  • Grinlo only moves when you are not watching him. He goes for the right door while your cameras are up.
  • Mimi hides in Curtain Cove. If you never use the cameras she gets restless and runs to the left door.
  • If a door button stops working, someone is already inside...
Headphones recommended. Click anywhere to enable sound.
